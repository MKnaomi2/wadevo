Philosophy

How we build

Respect over extraction

No dark patterns, no ads, no engagement traps. If a design needs a trick to keep you, it failed.

Privacy by architecture

On-device first. Your data stays yours because it never has to leave your hands.

Craft is the feature

Taste is not decoration. Every screen should feel considered, because the details are the product.
